# Session notes: 2026-09-28

Branch: `security-units-poc` (pushed; not merged to `main`).

## What we built today

### 1. Org chart: platoons, squads, duty roles
- Sign-up is now two steps: the unit code link is checked first ("Joining HHC..."), then the soldier picks **platoon → squad → duty role**. Only roles that fit that element are offered, and the server checks this too.
- Each unit starts from the standard **infantry rifle company template**: Company HQ; 1st–3rd Platoon (Platoon HQ, 1st–3rd Squad, Weapons Squad); Weapons Platoon (HQ, Mortar Section, Assault Section). The Readiness NCO can rename, add, or delete platoons and squads. Nothing with soldiers still in it can be deleted, and Company HQ can't be deleted.
- Org chart screen: leaders first, headcounts, vacancies. The NCO can tap anyone to move them, or place "Unassigned" people (anyone who signed up before this feature).
- Backend: `src/org/` (template and role catalog in `duty-roles.ts`).

### 2. Need-to-know on the org chart (stolen-phone protection)
Enforced on the **server**: data a viewer can't see is never sent to their phone.

| Viewer | Sees | Names |
|---|---|---|
| Commander, XO, 1SG, Platoon Leaders, Platoon Sergeants, Readiness NCO, admins | Whole company, including unassigned | Full ("SGT Felix Lopez") |
| Squad Leaders | Company HQ + own platoon | Full |
| Everyone else | Company HQ + own platoon | "SGT Lopez, F." |

Rules live in `backend/api/src/org/org-visibility.ts`. To confirm: Supply Sgt, Training NCO, and RTO count as non-leaders; the XO counts as leadership.

### 3. iPhone-first redesign
- New theme: green / brown / tan gradients, vibrant, with WCAG AA text contrast (4.5:1) on the header gradient. Light and dark mode. iOS transitions, scrolling, grouped lists, and settings-style icon badges. On iPhone, the San Francisco font is used automatically.
- **Home**: gradient header with rank + name, unit, and duty role · position; tile grid with Org Chart, My Unit, Memos, Resources, Jobs, Manage Unit (NCOs), and All Units (admins). Tap your initials to sign out.
- Sign-in and sign-up use a full-gradient screen with a card. The app is now named **"NJ Guard"** on the home screen (iOS and Android).
- Design system: `lib/core/theme.dart` and `lib/core/ui.dart`. Build new screens from these.

### 4. Resources & Benefits and Jobs
- **Resources**: New Jersey / National toggle, with the Military & Veterans Crisis Line (988, press 1) pinned at the top.
- **Jobs**: NJ Guard Federal Technician and AGR listings (NJ Dept. of Military Affairs vacancy page), HRO recruitment page, and a USAJOBS search.
- Links are served by the backend (`backend/api/src/resources/resources.data.ts`), so fixing a link doesn't need an App Store release. All links were checked against the live sites on 2026-09-28.
- NJ DMAVA split in Jan 2026 into **Military Affairs (`nj.gov/dma`)** and **Veterans Affairs (`nj.gov/dva`)**. Old `nj.gov/military` links are outdated.
